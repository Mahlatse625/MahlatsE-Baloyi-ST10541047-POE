About Student Stay SA

Organisation Overview

Student Stay SA is a proposed student accommodation information service designed to help students find suitable accommodation near their campus.

Mission

Our mission is to provide students with clear and useful accommodation information so they can make informed decisions about where to stay.

Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted starting point for students searching for convenient and affordable accommodation.

Target Audience

  • New university and college students.
  • Returning students looking for accommodation.
  • Parents or guardians assisting students.
  • Students who want accommodation close to campus.
